# Central Area

crime_data["DATE OCC"] = pd.to_datetime(crime_data["DATE OCC"])
crime_data["year"] = crime_data["DATE OCC"].dt.year

central_area = crime_data[crime_data["AREA NAME"] == "Central"]
year_count = central_area.groupby("year").size()

# line graph
plt.plot(year_count.index, year_count.values)

plt.title("Crime Rates in Central Area Overtime")
plt.xlabel("Year")
plt.ylabel("Number of Crimes")

plt.show()

The crime rates spiked during the pandemic in the Central area.¶

Conclusion¶

The Central area has the highest number of crimes of around 70,000. The number of crimes in the top 10 areas range between 50,000 - 70,000. There was an increase of crime rates closer to when the pandemic occured and some slightly decreased post-pandemic. The most common crime type to occur is stolen vehicles with nearly half as more crimes occured than the rest of crime types.¶
